Record a row before implementing or materially expanding a capability. `Custom` is permitted only
with a link or quote identifying explicit owner authorization.

| SQLite access | `klemens-morgenstern/sqlite` | Typed queries, prepared statements, non-throwing APIs, RAII transactions/savepoints, hooks and backup. | Active through the pinned overlay.
